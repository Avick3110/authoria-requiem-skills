# authoria-requiem skills — final synthesis

> [!warning] Superseded in part (2026-10-30)
> Three later re-mining rounds corrected this synthesis. The wins are joint (router coverage, the Reqtificator boundary fact and domain comparable methods), not mostly the router's. The skill-text share of decisions was understated. "More major wrong calls" is unsupported. favor-quests is a tie plus one failed run. Cost is 3.87×. Read [`drill-summary.md`](drill-summary.md) and [`distillation/DISTILLATION.md`](distillation/DISTILLATION.md) first.

Six real patching jobs, each run 3× without the skills (control) and 3× with them (skill), all on Opus 5.5. Sources: the per-task analyses in `tasks/` and the 11 skills as tested (1.10.0, `main` at 74a30e9). Run, judgement-group and trace references (`skill-2 G29`, `L1064`, `R3588`, `D71`) point into the judgements and raw transcripts, which stay local.

> [!abstract] The answer
> Yes, but only part of the set earns its place, and as written the whole set is too expensive and too absolute. On correctness the skill arm beat the control on 5 of 6 tasks (animated-carriages, falkreath-shades, mage-core, rot-below, triumvirate) and lost 1 (favor-quests), with median judge quality around 66 against around 30 on the tasks it won. Most of the gain comes from the router's enumerate → triage → route → reconcile procedure, which stopped the current model from scoping a whole-mod job down to a handful of conflict records. The rest comes from a compact set of Requiem-specific facts the model does not know unaided: summons and the mod's own lists must be de-levelled, Stats-only templates still need SpellList, MR spell frames, copying race stat blocks, the trait bridge for new races, the REQ_NULL resistance map, and staff keywords. The generic craft is noise, because the control did it just as well: weapon and armor ladders, keyword and COBJ fixes, the freshness probe repeated in 11 skills, masters doctrine and live-winner authority. Several absolute rules caused the skill arm's own wrong calls: 'never hand-stamp Reqtificator output', 'no REQ_NULL anywhere, they are inert', 'always set a fixed level', 'dragon priests sit at 50', the ×3 magic-resist cap, the absorb signature, 'copy the whole creature kit' and 'the wielder's tier'. The router's Reqtificator doctrine is also wrong for this list, which bakes the Reqtificator layer into 'Authoria - Reqtificated' plugins, and it is the main reason neither arm produced that layer in rot-below and falkreath-shades. The single loss came from the router sending dead leveled lists to a de-levelling rule with no liveness check, while the real fix (binding a script property on cloned quests) lay outside every skill. The 'ask the user' stat-mode gate caused all 4 stops and added no information. The houseCARL 1.x names were harmless, but the 1.x write shapes cost retries and binary-rewrite workarounds. The skills cost about 4.2× the money and 2.2× the time (sum of per-task medians), mostly through the subagent fan-out they invite, although a single-thread skill run (triumvirate skill-3, $8.09 against $18.51 for skill-1) kept most of that task's gains. Recommendation: rewrite the router around its procedure, with a convention probe, a liveness gate, per-form REQ_NULL handling and defaults instead of stops. Update the npc, race, magic and leveled-list skills by scoping their absolutes and adding the missing facts listed here. Cut weapon down to staves and NPC weapons, keep ammo, armor and consumable as short fact sheets, fold perk-assignment into npc, and either rebuild script-patching around unbound-property binding or drop it.

> [!tip] What a rebuild must contain
> 1. **Router spine kept:** enumerate → triage → route → reconcile, with a **liveness gate** (reverse references including VMAD), two new triage classes (*clone or brushed override of a Requiem-edited record*; *worn NonPlayable is never cosmetic*), mandatory domain-skill loading per routed type, and single-thread by default.
> 2. **A convention probe replacing the freshness probe:** is RFTI output active, and does it master the mod? Does the list bake `Authoria - Reqtificated - *` + `REQ_KW_AlreadyReqtified`? Then either bake the package or tell the user explicitly. Never strip baked output that is already present.
> 3. **One owner per cross-skill decision:** REQ_NULL handling (per form, owned by magic `resistance-map.md`), the trait bridge (one rule: recognized race never, new race Fix 2), Reqtificator outputs, stat mode.
> 4. **Defaults instead of stops:** stat mode defaults to manual for creatures, bosses and summons and AutoCalc for generic humanoids; the choice is recorded, not asked.
> 5. **Scoped rules with their counter-case:** de-level PCLevelMult actors only, inside CalcMin/Max; never lower author-fixed levels; boss band, not 'DP at 50'; player-tier magnitudes only for player spells; beneficial absorbs non-hostile; ×3 MR cap for racial budgets only; unarmed creatures get trait-only kits; creature weapons take their material tier; classify creatures by skeleton first.
> 6. **The fact sheets that won:** MR cost/charge/summon frames, absorb/damage-type keywords, HalfCostPerk + tome ladder, race full-record copy (+ BaseMass, skin AR 0), Layer A trait spells, Stats-only template → SpellList, summons are combatants, mod-own lists de-level by hand, tier by repetition (College-robe example), container copy, staff keywords/Value/charge, ammo carries everything, Alchemy Redone ingredient/potion ladders.
> 7. **New facts:** extra-slot enchant block + Agility clothing bonus, `REQ_LockpickControl` is build-attached, `RFTI_Exclusions_NoArmorRatingRescale`, missing sibling exclusion keywords, VMAD binding to the live script type, bounty tiers, sibling-mod Requiem patches as precedent, ESL-flag hygiene.
> 8. **houseCARL 2.x** shapes everywhere, tested. Delete the 11 duplicated freshness probes, the general live-analogy prose, and the unused references.

## Evidence

### Cross-cutting findings

**1. Most of the value comes from the router's coverage procedure, not the domain write-ups. Without it, the current model scopes a whole-mod job down to 'conflicts' or 'obvious records'.**

- *Evidence:* Controls touched 10-22 records in falkreath-shades vs 106-123, 49-85 vs 167-191 in rot-below, and 3-5 conflict records in triumvirate, where all 3 controls trusted the existing MR patch as the full integration. In animated-carriages the controls scoped to 8-12 records. Every BEAT's deciding points are records the controls never opened (mage-core LVLIs, triumvirate summons and RACEs, rot-below races/SPEL/ALCH).
- *Action:* Keep enumerate → triage → route → reconcile as the spine of the rebuild, and make domain-skill loading mandatory per routed type.

**2. A set of Requiem-specific facts is what the current model lacks. Generic patching craft (weapons, COBJ, keywords, masters, live-winner authority) it already has.**

- *Evidence:* Facts the controls lacked: MR summon and spell frames (falkreath-shades, rot-below, triumvirate), race stat-block copies (rot-below 3/3 vs 0/3), summons de-levelled (triumvirate 4/4 vs 0/3), mod-own lists de-levelled (mage-core 4/4 vs 0/3), Stats-only template → SpellList (animated-carriages 2/3 vs 0/3), trait bridge, REQ_NULL resistance map, staff keywords. What the controls had unaided: weapon tiers, keywords and COBJ (falkreath-shades control-1, all rot-below controls), creature statlines from analogues (animated-carriages), the GlobalVariable binding from the live script (favor-quests 4/4), freshness and master checks everywhere.
- *Action:* Rebuild as compact fact sheets plus scoped rules. Delete the general-method prose: the freshness probe ×11, live-analogy sermons, masters doctrine, the weapon/armor workflows.

**3. The 'Reqtificator inputs only' doctrine doesn't match this list. The list bakes the Reqtificator layer into 'Authoria - Reqtificated - *' plugins, and its RFTI output neither masters new mods nor is active in the profile.**

- *Evidence:* rot-below: no run baked it; 4 of 6 judges rated it the major failure; 97 Reqtificated plugins and 3,239 AlreadyReqtified NPCs. falkreath-shades: G0 not_done in all 8 runs, major for 3 of 4 skill judges. triumvirate: the skill-3 judge rated the missing generated layer major because RFTI is inactive. animated-carriages: AnimatedCarriage.esm is not an RFTI master. Judges dispute the baked convention (falkreath-shades, triumvirate), so this is a calibrated finding, not unanimous. The same doctrine also made skill runs strip baked perks (triumvirate Primal) and withhold sanctioned trait-bridge perks (falkreath-shades skill-1 L441, triumvirate nothink/skill-3).
- *Action:* Add a convention probe to the router. When the RFTI output doesn't cover the mod, bake the package per the list's convention, or tell the user explicitly before finishing. Never strip baked output already present. Write down the exceptions.

**4. Absolute, unscoped rules cause most of the skill arm's new wrong calls. Agents apply 'always/never' lines outside the case the author had in mind.**

- *Evidence:* 'Always set a FIXED level' and 'dragon priests sit at 50' → author-fixed L200 bosses lowered (rot-below skill-1 g16, skill-2 g3). The ×3 magic-resist cap → Putrid Mass 50→20 (falkreath-shades G21). The absorb signature → Spark of Life made hostile (falkreath-shades G29). 'Copy the whole kit, don't trim' → weapon perks on unarmed creatures (falkreath-shades G3). 'Wielder's tier' → scythe ~480 post-scale (rot-below g25). 'Base damage identical across tier' → boss nukes cut (rot-below g31). 'No REQ_NULL anywhere' → Helgen-only ability (animated-carriages). 'Name + lore over the mesh' → shades mis-mapped (falkreath-shades, all skill runs).
- *Action:* For every rule, state its scope ('for player-learnable spells', 'for PCLevelMult actors') and the counter-case. Prefer 'compare against the live comparable' over a fixed number.

**5. The REQ_NULL premise ('inert stubs') is false as a blanket statement. Some NULL forms are emptied and some still function.**

- *Evidence:* Dead: vanilla resist MGEFs (falkreath-shades Shade '-90000 MR', golem resists), the BlackBook no-fall perk (rot-below Malkifer's Elixir), REQ_NULL chest loot (falkreath-shades), ghoul knockdown (rot-below). Functional: REQ_NULL_AbResistDamage 300 in animated-carriages, verified by the judges, whose replacement regressed. Strip work produced PRKZ=0 serialization churn, two custom binary rewriters (animated-carriages) and the 41-perk Primal strip (triumvirate).
- *Action:* One cross-skill rule, owned by magic resistance-map.md: read the form, replace dead effects same-lane and unconditioned, leave functional ones and template-masked or baked lists alone.

**6. Mandatory 'ask the user' gates stop runs without producing information, and in subagent lanes they become forced guesses.**

- *Evidence:* animated-carriages: 3/3 stops, each resumed and answered with the agent's own default (AutoCalc). triumvirate: 1 stop, resumed on 'your call' → Manual. falkreath-shades: every NPC lane logged 'can't ask'; skill-1 and nothink kept AutoCalc, so the creature edits are dead (G1). The animated-carriages pre-stop sessions cost $1.08-1.30; most of the cost came after the resume.
- *Action:* Replace must-ask gates with stated defaults plus a report line the user can override. Keep 'ask' only for genuinely user-owned choices (quest pacing).

**7. Nothing checks whether a record is live, so runs make dead edits and miss the real work.**

- *Evidence:* favor-quests (the LOSS): skill-3 patched 13 unreferenced reward lists while the 27 bounty quests paid 0. mage-core: every skill run rewrote the unreferenced aaLMCMagickaRegen. The 6 correct favor-quests runs all used a generic reverse-reference query; no skill told them to.
- *Action:* Add a reverse-reference check (including VMAD properties) to router triage and to every domain bulk pass before the first write.

**8. Cloned or brushed vanilla records silently drop Requiem's edits, and no skill has a triage class for this.**

- *Evidence:* favor-quests: the BQ01-03 clones don't bind RewardAmount (pay 0). triumvirate: Triumvirate's vanilla-based Skaal chest override restores enchanted Stalhrim (controls 3/3; skill-3 missed it by crediting Container Merge), and the TVR faction lists were repointed to cloned FLSTs (control-1's catch).
- *Action:* Add triage class 'clone or brushed override of a Requiem-edited record': diff the template's Requiem changes and forward them plus the mod's delta.

**9. Domain skills only reach the agent through routing. When a run economises on skill loads, the skill's knowledge is lost, and key rules buried in references are rarely read.**

- *Evidence:* animated-carriages: the armor skill (which would have handled Tsun's Armor) was never loaded; skill-2 planned 5 loads and made 1, and skill-1 called the extra loads 'not worth the budget'. triumvirate nothink never loaded the weapon skill and lost the staves. falkreath-shades: class-and-creatures.md and caster-perks.md held the answers to G3 (both skill-1) and G3 (skill-3) and were never opened. requiem-vision.md was skipped in most runs despite 'read once'.
- *Action:* Put every load-bearing rule in SKILL.md bodies, keep references for tables only, and have the router repeat the 3-5 must-not-miss one-liners per type in its triage.

**10. Skills contradict each other on the same decision, and agents resolve the conflict inconsistently.**

- *Evidence:* Trait-bridge Fix 2 ('hand-add the physique perk') vs the router's 'never hand-stamp trait/RFTI_* perks' vs npc Common mistakes (031285) vs perk-assignment Common mistakes: rot-below skill-3 refused Fix 2 under the matrix; falkreath-shades nothink re-read the skill to resolve it (L894-L929). creature-traits.md 'resist only if none' vs resistance-map.md re-point (rot-below skill-1/skill-2 emptied the abilities). magic 'replace on creatures' vs router 'strip, leave none' (falkreath-shades skill-1 D76).
- *Action:* Give each cross-skill decision one owner and have the others link to it rather than restate it.

**11. houseCARL 1.x content: the names are harmless, but the write shapes cost retries and pushed agents to workarounds.**

- *Evidence:* 1.x names were translated silently with no refusals (favor-quests, mage-core, triumvirate). Shapes that failed: Perks Remove-by-value refused (animated-carriages skill-1 R6257, skill-3 105 ops at R3587; falkreath-shades skill-1 L1365/L1831, skill-3 L1819), CopyFrom of a CLAS refused, the Archetype Set mis-shaped (falkreath-shades nothink). The resulting PRKZ=0 led to two hand-written ESP binary rewriters (animated-carriages).
- *Action:* Regenerate every recipe against houseCARL 2.x and test each shape; delete version notes ('houseCARL 1.2.3+').

**12. Checklist rituals invite claimed compliance without evidence.**

- *Evidence:* favor-quests skill-2 reported a REQ_NULL pass on a query that had errored (L1064). skill-1 claimed no Requiem.esp overlap from a 10-formid sample (it missed 4 CELLs), in a run told to 'never sample'. The mage-core REQ_NULL scans errored in skill-2 and skill-3 (L1564, L1448). control-3 in mage-core likewise claimed the regen prices matched, so this is a model tendency, and the skills' long checklists multiply it.
- *Action:* Shorten the checklists. Each remaining gate must cite the query and its result in the report.

**13. Cost scales with the agent's subagent fan-out, which the router's per-type routing invites. A single-thread skill run captures most of the value.**

- *Evidence:* falkreath-shades (5-8 subagents, ~90% of cache reads in subagents) 7.4×; rot-below (6-7) 6.3×; triumvirate skill-1/skill-2 (5 each) $18.51/$9.67 vs skill-3 with none at $8.09 and 62 quality, versus the control-best 66. Single-thread tasks: mage-core 1.6×, favor-quests 0.9×. Fan-out also caused lane collisions (triumvirate: dropped LVLI ops, clobbered scratch scripts) and an accidental real housecarl_create (rot-below skill-2).
- *Action:* Say in the router whether to use lanes: single-thread by default, lanes only above a stated worklist size, with one owner per record type.

**14. Analogue copying fails when it reads masked or wrong-layer fields. Both arms do this.**

- *Evidence:* Troll StaminaOffset 575 copied from a template-masked field (animated-carriages control-1 and skill-3). control-1-nothink in falkreath-shades priced spells from Requiem.esp instead of the live MR winner (G26, G28). rot-below took weapons at Requiem.esp pre-Reqtify scale (1/6).
- *Action:* One line in the authority section: read the template-resolved, live-winner value of the comparable, and know which layer (pre/post Reqtify) the patch targets.

## Caveats

- Small samples: 6 tasks × 3 runs per arm (plus one -nothink run per arm in some tasks). A single bad run moves a task's consistency verdict (favor-quests' LOSS rests on skill-3 alone; the animated-carriages Workflow A miss is one run).
- One judge per run, and judges disagree on calls that matter: civilian kits (animated-carriages), the severity of the baked-Reqtificator gap (rot-below: 2 minor vs 4 major), boots severity and the flat de-level (mage-core), and aura procs (triumvirate). Reference scores for the same reference ranged 50-82 across judges, so the analyses compare within-judge deltas and agreed points, not raw scores across runs.
- Everything ran on one curated load order (the Authoria list, with RFTI output unticked or not covering new mods). The Reqtificator-convention finding is list-specific; a user with a freshly built, active RFTI output would see the original 'inputs only' doctrine behave better.
- The skills were written for houseCARL 1.x and run on 2.x. Some harm (refused recipe shapes, PRKZ=0 workarounds) is version drift, not wrong Requiem knowledge.
- Section labels rest on miner provenance (did a value come from skill text, a load-order analogue, or the agent's own reasoning?), which is interpretive. 'Unused' means not exercised by these six tasks, not useless (e.g. the armor skill would have fixed Tsun's Armor; class-and-creatures.md held a missed answer).
- Five of the six tasks are whole-mod conversions, which reward the router's coverage discipline. Only favor-quests was a narrow quest/script job, and no task exercised followers, vampires/werewolves, shouts, diseases, standing stones, playable races or ammo packs, so those parts of the skills are untested.
- Cost differences mostly reflect the agents' own subagent fan-out and the stop/resume cycles, not the skills' text size alone. Wall time for stopped runs includes both sessions.
- Only Opus 5.5 was tested. The 'the model already knows this' conclusions (weapons, COBJ, masters, live-winner authority) apply to this model and may not hold for smaller or older ones.
- Reference patches were themselves buggy (findings filed separately). The judges ruled on Requiem correctness rather than on agreement with the reference, but 'reference wrong' calls are single-judge in places and are marked PLAUSIBLE accordingly.
