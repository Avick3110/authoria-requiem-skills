# requiem-patching: what helped, what hurt, what was unnecessary

> [!summary] Headline
> The router earns its place through one thing: the **plan** (enumerate every record, triage with no drops, route each type to a domain skill, reconcile the count). That plan is the only router-only win in the eval (FS1 coverage, 4/4 vs 0/4), and router scope or trigger is half of 9 joint deciding points (8 with in-game value; FS3 is a no-op, R1). Its second lever is a **corrective boundary fact** in the matrix ("NPC: you carry fixed level, class, perks"). It is only half stated and needs the code-proven version. Almost all of the harm comes from four places: the REQ_NULL "inert stubs, none may remain" family, the blanket "never hand-stamp" line, a route with no liveness check (the favor-quests failed run, joint with the LL skill), and false or incomplete facts (lockpicking DLL, rp#203 werewolf trait; plus the unverified ×3 magic-resist budget misapplied to one creature). The "AR ~10×, capped" line is false but no skill-arm edit rested on it (R1). About 80% of the text is noise, untested, or duplicated.
> **Verdict: rewrite.** Keep the plan nearly verbatim and make reconciliation unskippable. Replace the doctrine with a code-proven generator contract and an upstream/downstream stance choice. Move the gap-mechanic references into a short Requiem profile, and move tool shapes and master mechanics to houseCARL. Target: about 90 lines of SKILL.md.

Evidence vs inference: the tables cite evidence. Lines marked *(inference)* are my joins.

## 1. What it is, and how much it was used

The integration router for a "patch mod X for Requiem" job. It enumerates the plugin, triages and routes every record type to one of the 10 domain skills, owns the gap mechanics no domain skill holds (vampirism, disease, stealth, economy, alchemy, food, shouts, stones, perks, combat model), and closes with a masters / REQ_NULL / Reqtificator checklist.
