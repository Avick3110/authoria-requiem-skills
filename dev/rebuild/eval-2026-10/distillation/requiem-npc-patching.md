> [!summary] Headline
> The NPC skill carries the most valuable domain method in the set, but almost all of it fits in ~15 lines. Its wins are **Workflow B (tick SpellList on Stats-only templates)**, **de-level PCLevelMult actors including summons and merchants**, **copy the live analogue's whole kit (perks + spells + trait)**, **manual stats for creatures/bosses**, and **trait-bridge Fix 2 for custom races**. Each won jointly with the router's boundary fact ("the Reqtificator does not set template flags, de-level, or give custom races traits"), and values came from the load order. Its harms are a stop-to-ask gate, three internal contradictions (stat mode vs creature offsets; "never hand-stamp" vs Fix 2; REQ_NULL strip vs "don't clean masked NULLs"), two unscoped absolutes (fixed level, boss numbers), one false premise (REQ_NULL = inert), one wrong label (Authoria - Reqtificated = tool output) and 1.x write shapes. About 85% of the 1,539 lines are duplicated, unused, generic craft, or houseCARL's job.

## 6. Rewrite brief

**Purpose.** Bring a mod's `NPC_` records onto Requiem's actor model as an **upstream input patch** (the Reqtificator runs later), and say plainly what a **downstream** bake would add, as a per-patch choice. Opus 5.5 does the generic craft unaided; the skill should hold only the boundary, the method of "which comparable, which fields", and the traps.

**Order of the procedure (new; fixes X2, X3, X17):** for each NPC, (1) combatant or civilian (combat signal first); (2) template state: Stats+SpellList → done once the chain ends at a Requiem-tuned record; Stats without SpellList → tick SpellList; no usable template → re-template onto a Requiem-tuned template or copy the analogue; (3) level: only PCLevelMult actors are de-levelled; (4) stats: per-actor mode; (5) kit; (6) custom race → Fix 2.

**Must keep (verbatim or near):**
1. R9 line: *"`Stats` without `SpellList` is not Workflow A: perks and spells travel only with `SpellList`, so a Stats-only combatant has nothing but its own list. Tick `SpellList` (Workflow B) once its own `ActorEffect` holds no modded spell, because stripping its REQ_NULL perks alone leaves it with no perks."* Put it at the template step, the bulk-pass triage and the drain check.
2. Boundary (3 lines, owned by the Requiem profile, quoted here): the Reqtificator does not tick template flags, does not de-level, does not give custom races trait perks.
3. "A summoned/conjured actor is a combatant; its NPC record gets a fixed level from the Magic Redone/Requiem summon analogue. The spell does not de-level it."
