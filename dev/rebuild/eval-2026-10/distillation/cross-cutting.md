# Cross-cutting distillation

> [!summary] Headline
> - **What helped across skills is three things, used together.** (1) The router's enumerate, then per-record disposition, then route, then reconcile. (2) One corrective boundary fact: "the Reqtificator does not set NPC template flags, de-level, add traits to custom races, or touch a mod's own LVLI". (3) A domain skill telling the agent which live comparable to read. Of 17 deciding points, 9 are joint, 1 is router-only (FS1) and 1 is domain-only (RB1) (R2). The values come from the load order, not from skill text.
> - **What hurt across skills is a short list of repeated mechanisms**: the REQ_NULL "inert stubs" family (false for every type but PERK, and a churn-and-close-the-task rule even for PERK; repeated in about 63 rule lines across all 11 skills); router lines used out of scope (cosmetic-skip, the race resist cap on one ability) or false (rp#169 on quests); the hand-stamp ban beating the trait-bridge exception; stop-to-ask gates (0 helped in 19 rows); checklist lines that turned into claims of compliance; lane briefs that paraphrased rules; routing before any liveness check; and write shapes houseCARL refuses or mangles.
> - **What was unnecessary is mostly duplication and generic craft**: the freshness probe (in 10 SKILL.md files, about 134 lines), 9 houseCARL recipe files (1,589 lines), 10 bulk-pass protocols (668 lines), 11 checklists (329 lines), and about 620 lines of worked examples, none of them used. Six domains were never tested. The rewrite should keep short versions of those, not cut them.
> - **Verdict:** rebuild the cross-cutting layer once: a small general layer, a Requiem profile that holds the boundary fact, REQ_NULL by type and the stance, and an ARR list layer for house conventions. Each cross-skill decision then has one owner (section 5). Remove every per-skill copy.

Conventions. Evidence = a cited item or ledger row. *Inference* is marked. Rule ids are from `drill/absolutes/requiem-*.json` (rp = requiem-patching). Judgement groups are written rNN gN (0-based unless the source says otherwise). Run ids: AC/FS/FQ/MC/RB/TR + c/s + run number, "n" = nothink.
